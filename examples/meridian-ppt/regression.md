# Worked example — Meridian Office Furniture (fictional, fully reproducible)

A synthetic company corpus, generated by `make_assets.py` (deterministic,
python-pptx only), with the phenomena a real corpus contains planted on
purpose: a corner-furniture spec, singleton display sizes, sub-floor
footnotes, auto-fit org-chart text, a near-brand color variant, and an
untouched default Office theme. Anyone can regenerate the corpus and
re-run the whole pipeline.

## Corpus

2 files, 9 slides, 59 sized runs, 13 distinct sizes (`fingerprint.json`).

- Corner cluster found cold: (0.35, 0.32, 0.28×0.28)in on 4 slides — exactly
  the planted spec.
- Text colors expose the planted variant: brand navy `1F3864` ×14 runs vs
  near-navy `203A66` ×1 run — a decision item, not a silent merge.
- Theme declares the Office default; the brand exists only in direct
  formatting (usage outranks declarations).

## Derivation (raw, no human input)

6 whitelist sizes (10/10.5/12/16/18/40), directly covering 79.7% of runs;
5 auto-fit runs (11.41 ×3, 9.85 ×2) classified, not whitelisted;
sub-floor decision item 7.0pt ×3 (footnotes); singleton decision items
20 / 24 / 28 / 54pt.

## Verdicts (generated checker, `--asset-mode`)

| Stage | meridian-template | meridian-library |
|---|---|---|
| Raw derived whitelist | **FAIL** (4 errors) | PASS |
| After decision-list sign-off (+20/24/28/54) | PASS | PASS |

Every raw-stage error is one of the four singleton decision items (TOC
heading 20, chapter title 24, closing 28, chapter numeral 54) — the forge
refusing to silently standardize one-off display sizes. The 7pt footnotes
pass in asset mode without whitelisting, matching the checker's
template-furniture rule. Negative control: a deck with a color block
partially covering text FAILs under the generated checker.

## Reproduce

```bash
pip install python-pptx
python3 examples/meridian-ppt/make_assets.py /tmp/meridian-assets
python3 scripts/extract_fingerprint.py /tmp/fp.json /tmp/meridian-assets/*.pptx
python3 scripts/derive_whitelist.py /tmp/fp.json --out /tmp/w.json --decisions /tmp/d.md
```
