# PPT Skill Forge

English | [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

Turn a company's existing PowerPoint assets — the official template, seed decks, page libraries, a pile of historical decks — into a company-specific PPT skill: an enforceable brand base, a declared font-size whitelist, and delivery gates that check every new deck automatically.

## Why this exists

Most companies *have* a presentation style; almost none have it in a form an AI agent (or a new hire) can execute and verify. The style is trapped in template files and in the habits of whoever made last quarter's deck, while the declared theme usually still says "Office default". This skill extracts the real standard from observed usage, puts the genuinely ambiguous findings in front of a human as one batched decision list, and generates a self-contained skill package whose checkers enforce the result.

It is the presentation-domain companion to [company-knowledge-to-skill](https://github.com/ryangucheng2022-sudo/company-knowledge-to-skill) (terminology and business conventions) and [business-agent-skill-builder](https://github.com/ryangucheng2022-sudo/business-agent-skill-builder) (business workflows): knowledge → presentations → processes.

## How it works

| Stage | What happens | Human checkpoint |
|---|---|---|
| Inventory | Classify the asset files; stratify the corpus by page type | Corpus gaps flagged before anything is derived |
| Fingerprint | Extract from observed usage: font-size histogram, fonts, solid fills, corner-furniture position clusters; theme recorded for contrast | — |
| Derive | Three-layer rule produces a whitelist candidate + decision list | **Sign off the decision list** — sub-floor sizes, singletons, near-brand colors |
| Generate | Assemble the company package: SKILL.md, generic checkers, `font_sizes.json`, fingerprint + decision references, assets | Confirm the brand-base section |
| Verify | Generated checker over the company's own assets + a constructed-defect deck | Verdicts must match; defects must be caught |

Design principles:

- **Usage outranks declarations.** Real brands live in direct formatting, not in the theme. In the worked example, the template's theme is the Office default while the actual brand (fonts, two near-identical navies, a corner mark) exists only in shapes.
- **Derived ≠ standard.** Singletons and sub-floor sizes are never silently whitelisted. A hand-set corner spec was rediscovered by clustering to within 0.01in — but it still goes through sign-off before becoming a spec.
- **Auto-fit is a class, not an error.** Non-quarter sizes hugging the underside of a declared size are auto-shrink artifacts; the system reports and tolerates them instead of bloating the whitelist.
- **Checkers are configuration-driven.** All company specificity lives in one config file; checking logic is copied unchanged into the generated package.

## What's inside

- `SKILL.md` — the method: five stages, six trust rules, honest boundaries.
- `references/method.md` — the five stages in detail.
- `scripts/extract_fingerprint.py` — style fingerprint from observed usage: font sizes, fonts, fills, text colors, corner-furniture clusters; theme recorded for contrast.
- `scripts/derive_whitelist.py` — three-layer whitelist derivation plus the human decision list.
- `scripts/forge_skill.py` — assembles the company skill package.
- `scripts/check_consistency.py` — derivation determinism, forge smoke test, checker-logic integrity, corpus reproducibility.
- `templates/generated-skill.md` — the generated package's SKILL.md template.
- `assets/checkers/` — the generic geometry/content checkers copied into generated packages (logic unchanged; all specificity in the config).
- `examples/meridian-ppt/` — the fictional, reproducible worked example.
- `ci/` — a ready-to-enable CI kit.

## Who is it for

- Brand and presentation owners who have templates but no enforceable standard.
- Operations / PMO teams standardizing recurring management-reporting decks.
- Consultants who need to stand up a client's deck standard quickly — and defend how each rule was derived.
- Agent builders creating a company-specific PPT skill alongside a knowledge skill.

Typical scenarios: onboarding an AI agent to a company's deck style; auditing a legacy template before a rebrand; freezing a font-size whitelist before a heavy reporting season.

## Quick start

```bash
pip install python-pptx
python3 scripts/extract_fingerprint.py fingerprint.json path/to/assets/*.pptx
python3 scripts/derive_whitelist.py fingerprint.json --out font_sizes.json --decisions decisions.md
# → review and sign off decisions.md, adjust font_sizes.json
python3 scripts/forge_skill.py --company "Acme" --fingerprint fingerprint.json \
    --whitelist font_sizes.json --decisions decisions.md \
    --assets-dir path/to/assets --out ./acme-ppt-skill
```

## Worked example (fictional, reproducible)

`examples/meridian-ppt/` ships a synthetic company corpus (generated by a script, so anyone can reproduce it) with the messy phenomena of real corpora planted on purpose: raw derivation produces 6 whitelist sizes and holds 4 singleton display sizes as decision items; pre-sign-off, the template fails on *exactly* those four; after sign-off both files pass, and a constructed defect is still caught. Full record: `examples/meridian-ppt/regression.md`. The pipeline was additionally validated against a production enterprise skill's assets during development, with verdict equivalence after sign-off.

## CI

A ready-to-enable kit lives in `ci/`: copy `ci/ci.yml` to `.github/workflows/ci.yml` and every push and PR runs the consistency check — derivation determinism, forge smoke test, checker-logic integrity, and worked-example corpus reproducibility. See `ci/README.md`.

## Honest boundaries

The forge builds the *checking* layer completely; the generated skill's *generation* layer starts as a skeleton (generic page types + seed cloning). Content conventions — title rules, terminology — cannot be extracted from files; mine them as candidates and decide them with company-knowledge-to-skill.
